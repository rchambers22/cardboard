Privacy Policy

Cardboard — Last updated: June 2026

Cardboard is a board game catalog and social gaming app. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights over it.

1. What we collect

When you use Cardboard, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address, display name, and encrypted password, provided when you register.
  • Game data — games you add to your collection, ratings, play logs, session history, and group memberships.
  • Usage data — session RSVPs, votes, wishlist entries, and in-app feedback you submit.
  • Device tokens — a push notification token so we can send you session reminders and group activity alerts. This token is tied to your account and device, not to any personal identity.

We do not collect your location, contacts, browsing history, or any data from other apps on your device.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the Cardboard service:

  • Authenticating your account and keeping your session active.
  • Displaying your game collection, groups, and play history to you and to other members of groups you have joined.
  • Sending push notifications for events you have opted into (new sessions, RSVPs, voting, session reminders).
  • Reviewing feedback you submit to improve the app.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to providing Cardboard.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with the services required to operate the app:

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — our database and authentication provider. Data is stored in the EU (Ireland, AWS eu-west-1). Supabase is GDPR compliant.
  • Expo / EAS (expo.dev) — used to deliver push notifications via Expo Push Notification Service. Tokens are transmitted securely and not stored by Expo beyond delivery.
  • BoardGameGeek / Geekdo (boardgamegeek.com) — game title, cover art, and metadata are fetched from BGG's public API when you search for or add a game. No personal data is sent to BGG.

4. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (via the Profile tab in the app), all your personal data — including your profile, game collection, play logs, group memberships, and feedback submissions — is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

5. Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure — delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile tab, or by contacting us.
  • Portability — request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing where we rely on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

6. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Database access is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — each user can only read and write their own data, or data in groups they are members of.

7. Children

Cardboard is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has registered, please contact us and we will delete the account.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
